Demonstration video script — one speaker

Group code MOB-G03-7014. One person holds the phone, speaks, and taps. One continuous, unedited recording. Maximum 90 seconds. Aim for about 80. The status-bar clock stays visible the whole time.

At the start, say your own name, registration number, and role. Practice the typing until the valid form takes under 15 seconds. Keep this beside the phone: Mama Keza, MZ-A-014, Produce, +250 788 123 456, High.
